A record of what we observed

These dates show when the tracker observed a documentation change, which can be later than when Microsoft made it. A newly discovered article is new to this tracker; it doesn’t necessarily announce a new feature.

Cards show changed text excerpts or reviewed plain-language comparisons, not an assessment of product behavior or what you need to do. Open the exact change or the current Microsoft Learn article for the full context.

Metadata includes document ownership, descriptions, dates, and classifications. Enable “Include metadata changes” to browse these updates separately from article text. First captures are labeled and do not establish when those values changed.

Scheduled checks at 9 a.m., 3 p.m., and 9 p.m. Eastern; publication can be delayed.
